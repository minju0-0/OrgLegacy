Sample entries

Every entry belongs to a volume.

Terms are the spine of the record, so anything can be traced to the officers who made it.

Closed volumes stay closed.

Sealed terms can be read by anyone with access, and edited by no one.

Closed door

No public directory. Join by code only.

There is no listing to browse and no request-to-join button. If an officer hasn't handed you an accession number, the archive doesn't know you exist.

Roles

Three seats. One shared memory.

  • Member

    Redeems an accession number and adds to the records of their committee.

  • Committee Head

    Leads a committee and writes its handover notes.

  • Executive

    Charters the collection and signs off each volume.
