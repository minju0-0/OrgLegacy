What the record holds

Everything the last officers knew, in one place.

Events and budgets

Supplier memory

{% include "shared/_icon.html" with name="note" %}

Handover notes

Outgoing officers write to the people who follow them, filed by committee and kept for good.

How it works

An academic year, from open to sealed.

Every A.Y. moves through three states, and only an executive can move it forward.

  1. ActiveOfficers log the A.Y. as it happens.
  2. Pending sign-offExecutives review before anything closes.
  3. ClosedSealed onto the shelf. Read, never rewritten.

  1. Charter

    An executive charters the organization and opens its first A.Y.

  2. Join by code

    Members redeem an accession number handed over by an officer.

  3. Log

    Events, budgets, suppliers and lessons go on the record.

  4. Hand over

    Outgoing officers write notes for the people who follow.

  5. Sign off

    Executives approve, and the A.Y. is sealed.
