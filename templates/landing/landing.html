How it works

An A.Y., from open to closed.

Every A.Y. moves through three states, and only an executive moves it forward.

  1. ActiveOfficers log the A.Y. as it happens.
  2. Awaiting approvalExecutives review it before it closes.
  3. ClosedFiled on the shelf. Anyone can read it, no one can change it.

  1. Create

    An executive creates the organization and opens its first A.Y.

  2. Join by code

    Members join with a code handed over by an officer.

  3. Record

    Events, budgets, suppliers and lessons go on the record.

  4. Hand over

    Outgoing officers write notes for the people who follow.

  5. Approve

    Executives approve, and the A.Y. is closed.

Everything filed. Nothing overwritten.
